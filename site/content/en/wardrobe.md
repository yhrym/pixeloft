Everyone has a character that sits at their desk. You change its look from **Dress up** in the office.

## Colors and choices

- Free colors for hair, clothes and trousers.
- Built-in hairstyles, outfits and accessories.
- Skin tone, and desk decorations from **Decorate desk**.
- Emotes (dance, clap, heart and others) that play above your character for a few seconds.

## Shared parts

Hairstyles, outfits and accessories can also be drawn by members. Open **Parts** in the menu (`/parts`). Parts are shared with everyone, and appear under **Shared parts** in the dress-up panel.

### Drawing a part

Choose **Draw a part**, pick a kind (hairstyle, outfit or accessory), give it a name of 1 to 20 characters, and draw inside the dashed box.

- **Tools**: pencil, eraser, eyedropper, clear, and copy front to back.
- **Front and back**: you draw both sides. Tick "Draw the back too" for a back view; otherwise the front is used.
- **Palette**: role colors, which follow whatever colors the wearer has chosen (hair color, clothes, trousers), and a fixed palette for colors that must not change.
- **Overhang, vertical position, hat**: hairstyles can extend past the head by up to 4 columns per side. An accessory marked as a hat hides the hair under it.
- **Preview**: shows the part on a seated character from the front and the back while you draw.

An outfit is 16 columns by 6 rows. Hairstyles and accessories have up to 20 rows.

### Limits and permissions

- Up to 50 parts per member.
- The creator and admins can edit or delete a part. Anyone can use it.
- If a part is deleted, people wearing it go back to the default look for that item.
- **Copy and edit** makes your own copy of someone else's part.
